checkers, played as a sequencer

Standard American checkers (diagonal moves, mandatory jumps, kinging) with a twist: every move sweeps a bar once across the eight columns, left to right, and sings whatever pieces are sitting on each column as it passes. Row picks the pitch, color picks the register, and the skin you choose is the instrument -- every game becomes a score no two players will ever repeat.

Sound needs a tap to start (browser rule, not ours).
