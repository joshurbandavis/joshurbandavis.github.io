Bacterial Bloom

The sibling of Cellular Erasure, run on Lenia instead of Conway's Game of Life: a continuous field standing in for the binary grid, growing and dying back like a culture in a dish instead of flipping cells on and off. It doesn't settle the way Life does — it breathes, drifts, sometimes dies back to nothing, sometimes overgrows everything at once. Every word's weight, blur and color track the field's value under it in real time. It doesn't need to settle to give up a poem, though: Capture this bloom takes whatever the field looks like right now and thresholds it against its own average brightness into a Selected reading — the same "lift the survivors out" move Cellular Erasure makes when it settles, just taken on your call instead of the automaton's.
