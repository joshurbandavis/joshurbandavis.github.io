chess, played as a sequencer

Full standard chess (check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant, promotion) with every move sweeping a bar once across the eight files, left to right. Piece type picks the instrument, color picks the register, rank picks the pitch, and the skin you choose sets the whole palette -- the same game becomes a different piece of music entirely.

Sound needs a tap to start (browser rule, not ours).
