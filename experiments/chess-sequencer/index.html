chess, in relation

Every position has pressure, protection and silence. Move a piece and hear the relationships change. Colored outlines reveal what the music responds to: attacked pieces add tension, protected pieces sustain, and crowded pieces become quieter. Full chess rules apply; piece type sets the instrument, color sets its register, and the sound palette is yours to choose.

Sound needs a tap to start (browser rule, not ours).
