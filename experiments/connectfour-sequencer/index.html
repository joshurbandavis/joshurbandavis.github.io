connect four, played as a sequencer

Standard Connect Four (drop a disc into any of the seven columns, first to line up four wins) with a twist: every drop sweeps a bar once across the columns, left to right, and sings whatever pieces are stacked in each one as it passes. Row picks the pitch, color picks the register, and the skin you choose is the instrument -- every game becomes a score no two players will ever repeat.

Sound needs a tap to start (browser rule, not ours).
