A note on the glass +

A study for a monumental mirror on the open playa: blackened metal, weathered brass, crescent crown, cast moths, and a reflection with a will of its own. The desert view is a visual scale study, not a construction plan.

The transformations happen automatically. Try moving slowly, holding still, lifting a hand, or inviting someone to join you. Stillness makes the celestial reading brighter.

For the physical sculpture, the central illusion is a recognizable human presence that quietly becomes impossible. The broad silhouettes, luminous moths, and shared heart are meant to read across a dark open space; the fine linework rewards coming closer.

Camera silhouettes are estimated locally using MediaPipe. The Union warps the combined silhouette; it does not identify or count individual visitors. Preview mode uses animated stand-ins. This browser study does not simulate projection brightness, daylight optics, wind, or dust protection.

The vision library and model load from jsDelivr and Google. No camera images leave this page.
