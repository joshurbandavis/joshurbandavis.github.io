the house band

The six sequencers, left on. Two engines play each other forever, a different game every ten minutes: chess on the hour, then checkers, reversi, connect four, mancala and backgammon. Nothing is streamed. Every move is worked out from the clock, so everyone tuned in hears the same move at the same moment.
