the house band

The six sequencers, left on. Every game is always being played, engine against engine, each at its own tempo, so their sweeps drift in and out of phase. Choose which members are on: one alone is a solo, all six is a wash. Nothing is streamed. Every move is worked out from the clock, so everyone hears the same games at the same moment, each mixed their own way.
