gygès, played as a sequencer

Gygès: 12 shared rings -- four each of height 1, 2 and 3 -- sit on a 6×6 board with no fixed owner. Each turn you must move a ring from your own front-most occupied row, in a straight line exactly as many spaces as its height. Land on an empty space and your move is done; land on another ring and you choose -- bounce it onward exactly its own height (which may cascade further), or replace it anywhere empty on the board. Get any ring off the far edge into your opponent's goal and you win. The twist here: every move sweeps a bar once across the six columns, left to right, and sings whatever's sitting on each column as it passes -- row picks the pitch, a ring's own height picks the register, and the skin you choose is the instrument.
