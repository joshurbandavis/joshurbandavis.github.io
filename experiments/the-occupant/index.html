An interactive artwork: a small creature that lives inside this browser tab and slowly learns what exists beyond its rectangle. Move your mouse or finger, resize the window, switch tabs and come back, press enter on the creature to interact, click the small glowing points that appear near the edges, drag its popup windows by their title bar toward a corner, or choose what shape it might be: it has never seen itself. It reacts on its own over time even without input.
