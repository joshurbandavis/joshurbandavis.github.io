01 — About

Systems for
strange futures

I'm a designer and engineer working at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and human-computer interaction. Based in San Francisco, I build multimodal systems that push on questions of creativity, communication, and who gets to participate in both.

My research has received best paper awards at C&C and ASSETS, honorable mentions at CHI, and recognition from the Neukom Institute. My creative practice has been supported by a grant from the Queer Arts Council.

Work exhibited at Grey Area Foundation for the Arts, D!iverseWorks, Synaps Projects, the Blaffer Art Museum, the Chandler Center for the Arts, and at the Venice Architecture Biennale in 2021.

I live with my two cats, Jynx and Nyx, in San Francisco.

03 03 — Design

Past & ongoing
works

01

Deleted World Tarot

A fully illustrated 80-card tarot deck and guidebook. Many influences went into this deck, from emerging technologies and internet cultures to occult histories and tarot illustrations past.

The early internet promised unlimited connection; that vision faded into disinformation and data-driven oppression. A choice was made. A world was deleted.

Includes a hand-illustrated companion book, Atlas of Deleted Worlds, custom marbled box, and printed ephemera.

Purchase Press Paper
02

if you go_say hello

A memorial: an AI trained on a deceased poet's writing, attached to a satellite, sent into orbit. Twice daily, visitors received transmissions. Over months, cosmic radiation corrupted the computer. Poems degraded to noise, then silence.

Venice Architecture Biennale, 2021.

03

The Night Air

A collaborative poem composed by visitors during COVID-19 lockdown, inspired by miasma, the medieval belief that bad air spread plague. Technology as connection, language as survival.

Houston Arts Alliance grant.

04

Selected Works

Experiments in photomanipulation, shader rendering, generative neural networks, and digital collage — spanning series including A Sudden Rush of Blood to the Skin, Hideouts, and Postcards from the Electric Void.

When the glitch arrives, it's the most human thing in the frame.

Instagram C&C NeurIPS
05

Synapstraction

A brain-computer installation: EEG headsets measure your neural response to five stimuli, one per sense, each mapped to a painting implement through machine learning. Your brain serves as conduit.

Black Visual Arts Center, 2017.

06

thisObituaryDoesNotExist

StyleGAN and GPT-2 imagine photographs, death narratives, and life stories for people who never existed. Printed as funeral pamphlets indistinguishable from real ones.

Visit
07

Psych-VR

VR relaxation content for astronauts in isolated spaces, developed with NASA and the Geisel School of Medicine. "Three Days and a Year On Top of a Mountain" compresses a year of timelapse footage into a single sunrise-to-sunset experience.

08

Animation

Ongoing experiments since 2017: abandoned sculptures, lab timelapse, visual programming. Each film paired with contemporary literature, soundscaped in SuperCollider.
