the night air is a community poem built the way the French surrealists built Exquisite Corpse — everyone contributes a small piece blind, without seeing where anyone else's fits, and the whole only exists once it's read together. Answer the five prompts below in a word or two each. They'll be woven into the growing poem alongside everyone who's answered since 2020.
